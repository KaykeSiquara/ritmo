# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who run their day from a phone: checking and ticking tasks for a few seconds at a time between commitments, often in daylight and on the move, and planning at a desk now and then. They want to capture a thought before it slips and to feel the day moving forward.

## Product Purpose
Ritmo keeps tasks, focus sessions and habits in one place, and makes adding a task as fast as typing the sentence you already have in your head. Success is a day that feels in motion: the list shrinks, the streak grows, and nothing important is forgotten.

## Brand Personality
Energetic, motivating, alive. Confident and encouraging without cheerleading. Short, active copy; progress is shown, not praised.

## Anti-references
The Prumo documentation site it is built on: the same neutral blue, system font and boxed cards would make it read as a component demo instead of a product. Also the generic SaaS card kit, cream-and-serif AI layouts and gamified confetti.

## Design Principles
- The thumb comes first: primary actions sit within reach on a phone.
- Show momentum: progress, streaks and completion are visible and felt.
- Every list has an identity: an icon and a color that make it findable at a glance.
- Motion reports a change of state, never decoration.
- Built on Prumo, dressed as Ritmo: the design system's behavior and accessibility stay, the identity is the product's own.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Every text pair is contrast-tested in both themes, color is never the only signal, touch targets reach 44 px on touch screens, every action works from the keyboard, and reduced motion turns animation into instant changes.
